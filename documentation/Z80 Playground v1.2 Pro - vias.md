## Notes

 - pGc01's 68 is far from the 53 vias of RCBUS80 RT2, from which this design is "derived".
 - pGd02's 73 is far from pGc01's 68 (but much better than pGd01's 86
 - pGd07 has a respectable 63 vias, but that is still 10 more than RCBUS80 RT2.
 - The Pro is *meant* to have ***less vias*** than the RCBUS80 RT2, from which it is derived.
